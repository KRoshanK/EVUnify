# EVUnify — Research Findings (October 2026)

| OEM                  | Approx. EV Market Share | Primary App(s)                          | Charging Approach / Scale                          | Notes |
|----------------------|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|-------|
| **Tata Motors**     | ~43%                   | iRA.ev (Charge Point Aggregator); Tata Power EZ Charge | Tata Power EZ Charge (~5,500–6,700+ public points); aggregates 15+ CPOs | Strongest national footprint; Mega Chargers |
| **Mahindra**        | ~22%                   | Charge_iN                              | Aggregates multiple operators; ultra-fast focus, real-time, payments | Dedicated charging app |

## Independent / Major CPOs
Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, ChargeZone, BPCL Pulse, Jio-bp Pulse, Shell Recharge, Adani, HPCL, ChargeMOD, Relux, Zeon, and many regional players.

Total public stations: tens of thousands and growing rapidly under PM E-DRIVE and related schemes.

## Existing Aggregators
- MG e-Hub
- MyMotor (30+ networks, one wallet)
- ChargeIndia (40+ CPOs, UPI payments)
- VoltRoute, ChargeRadar, NextCharge (early)
- Open Charge Map (partial Indian coverage)

## Government Efforts
- EV Yatra (BEE): Discovery portal/app (limited real-time and payments).
- Unified Bharat e-Charge / Super App under PM E-DRIVE (BHEL involvement): Aims for one platform for locate/book/pay across operators.

## Google Maps Gap
Many stations not systematically listed as public POIs. Google supports EV charging search and plug-type filters, but coverage and freshness in India are incomplete.

## Key Insight
OEMs are already aggregating CPOs. The opportunity is a neutral, Maps-first aggregator that improves discovery and navigation without replacing operator payment/session flows.
